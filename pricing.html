Pricing FAQ

Important details before starting a project.

Are these fixed prices?

No. These are estimated ranges to help you understand potential costs. Your final quote will depend on the features, complexity, and requirements of your project.

Can I request a completely custom project?

Yes. You can ask about Python applications, Discord bots, automation, or scripting in other programming languages. Custom projects are quoted individually.

Does the price include hosting?

Not automatically. Hosting, domains, paid APIs, databases, and other third-party services may have separate costs. These details should be agreed upon before the project starts.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Small corrections to the agreed requirements can be discussed as part of the project. New features or changes beyond the original scope may require an additional quote.

How do I get a quote?

Join the DevKaz Discord server and describe your idea. Include the features you need and any important requirements so the project can be estimated more accurately.
